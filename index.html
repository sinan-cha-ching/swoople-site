one swipe — every robot obeys

Swoople is a cozy sliding puzzle with one rule: every swipe moves every robot at once, and each one slides until something stops it. Park every robot on its own dock to win. Easy to feel in ten seconds, endlessly tricky to master.

The chapters

482 hand-tuned levels across 20 chapters. New tricks arrive one at a time — heavy robots that barely budge, doors held open by pressure plates, icy docks with no grip, portals, open pits, and contrary robots that slide the wrong way — then start combining. Every level is solvable in a known number of moves; beating that number earns the stars that open new chapters.

The daily

One puzzle per day, the same for the whole world, flipping at a single global moment. One attempt, no hints — win it and your streak grows, with a history of how close to perfect each day was. Miss it and the streak is gone until tomorrow.

Good to know

  • Made for one hand and short sessions — no timers, no pressure
  • Works fully offline; progress stays on your device
  • Optional ads support the game — Remove Ads is a one-time purchase
  • Undo, hints, and retries when you want them; a pure puzzle when you don't
